BANKING FAQ - SYNTHBANK CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

=== PERSONAL LOANS ===

Q: What is a personal loan?
A: A personal loan is an unsecured loan provided to individuals for personal use such as medical expenses, home renovation, travel, education, or debt consolidation. It does not require collateral.

Q: What is the interest rate for personal loans?
A: SynthBank offers personal loan interest rates starting from 10.5% per annum up to 18% per annum depending on your credit score, income, and loan tenure. Customers with a credit score above 750 are eligible for the lowest rates.

Q: What is the maximum personal loan amount?
A: You can borrow between Rs. 50,000 and Rs. 40,00,000 as a personal loan. The exact amount depends on your monthly income, existing obligations, and credit history.

Q: What documents are required for a personal loan?
A: Required documents include: PAN card, Aadhaar card, last 3 months salary slips, last 6 months bank statements, Form 16 or ITR for the last 2 years, and a passport-size photograph.

Q: How long does it take to get a personal loan approved?
A: Personal loan approval typically takes 1-3 business days after submission of all documents. Disbursement happens within 24 hours of approval.

Q: What is the tenure for personal loans?
A: Personal loans are available for tenures ranging from 12 months to 60 months (1 to 5 years).

Q: Can I prepay my personal loan?
A: Yes, you can prepay your personal loan after 6 EMIs. A prepayment charge of 2-4% on the outstanding principal may apply.

=== CREDIT CARDS ===

Q: What credit cards does SynthBank offer?
A: SynthBank offers four credit cards: SynthClassic (entry level), SynthSilver (rewards), SynthGold (premium), and SynthPlatinum (super premium with travel benefits).

Q: What is the annual fee for credit cards?
A: SynthClassic: Rs. 500/year, SynthSilver: Rs. 999/year, SynthGold: Rs. 2,499/year, SynthPlatinum: Rs. 5,000/year. Annual fees are waived if annual spending exceeds the respective threshold.

Q: What is the credit limit on credit cards?
A: Credit limits range from Rs. 20,000 (SynthClassic) to Rs. 10,00,000 (SynthPlatinum) based on income, credit score, and card type.

Q: How do I apply for a credit card?
A: You can apply online via the SynthBank app or website, or visit any branch. You need PAN card, Aadhaar, income proof, and a credit score of at least 700.

Q: What rewards do I earn on credit card spending?
A: SynthSilver: 2 reward points per Rs. 100 spent. SynthGold: 4 points per Rs. 100. SynthPlatinum: 10 points per Rs. 100 + lounge access at 300+ airports. 1 reward point = Rs. 0.25.

Q: What is the grace period for credit card bill payment?
A: SynthBank offers a 20-day grace period from the statement date. No interest is charged if the full amount is paid within this period.

Q: What is the interest rate on credit card outstanding?
A: If the full due is not paid by the due date, an interest rate of 3.49% per month (41.88% annually) is charged on the outstanding balance.

Q: How do I block my credit card if it is lost or stolen?
A: Call our 24/7 helpline at 1800-XXX-XXXX, use the SynthBank mobile app to instantly block the card, or send an SMS "BLOCK XXXX" (last 4 digits) to 567678.

=== HOME LOANS ===

Q: What is the interest rate for home loans?
A: Home loan interest rates start from 8.5% per annum. Rates are linked to the repo rate and reviewed quarterly. Women applicants get an additional 0.05% concession.

Q: What is the maximum home loan amount?
A: SynthBank finances up to 90% of the property value for loans up to Rs. 30 lakhs, 80% for Rs. 30-75 lakhs, and 75% for loans above Rs. 75 lakhs.

Q: What is the tenure for home loans?
A: Home loans are available for up to 30 years, subject to the applicant's age not exceeding 70 years at loan maturity.

Q: What are the processing fees for home loans?
A: Processing fee is 0.5% of the loan amount, minimum Rs. 5,000 and maximum Rs. 15,000 (inclusive of GST).

=== FIXED DEPOSITS ===

Q: What are the FD interest rates at SynthBank?
A: FD rates range from 4.5% (7-29 days) to 7.25% (2-3 years). Senior citizens get an additional 0.50% on all tenures.

Q: What is the minimum amount for a fixed deposit?
A: Minimum FD amount is Rs. 1,000. There is no maximum limit.

Q: Can I take a loan against my FD?
A: Yes, you can get a loan up to 90% of your FD value at an interest rate of 1% above the FD rate.

=== GENERAL BANKING FAQ ===

Q: How do I reset my net banking password?
A: Visit the login page, click "Forgot Password", enter your registered mobile number and PAN, verify OTP, and set a new password.

Q: What is IMPS, NEFT, and RTGS?
A: IMPS: Immediate Payment Service — instant 24/7 transfers up to Rs. 5 lakhs. NEFT: National Electronic Funds Transfer — batch processing, 30-minute cycles, no amount limit. RTGS: Real Time Gross Settlement — instant for amounts above Rs. 2 lakhs.

Q: What are the NEFT/RTGS/IMPS charges?
A: IMPS: Rs. 5 for up to Rs. 1 lakh, Rs. 15 for Rs. 1-2 lakhs. NEFT: Free for amounts up to Rs. 10,000; Rs. 5 for Rs. 10,000-1 lakh. RTGS: Rs. 25 for Rs. 2-5 lakhs; Rs. 50 above Rs. 5 lakhs.

Q: How do I link my Aadhaar to my bank account?
A: You can link Aadhaar via net banking (Profile > Link Aadhaar), mobile app, visiting a branch, or calling customer care.

=== KYC AND COMPLIANCE ===

Q: What is KYC and why is it required?
A: KYC (Know Your Customer) is a mandatory RBI requirement to verify customer identity and address. It prevents fraud, money laundering, and financial crimes.

Q: Which documents are accepted for KYC?
A: Identity proof: PAN card, Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License. Address proof: Aadhaar, Passport, Utility bills (not older than 3 months), Rental agreement.

Q: How often do I need to update KYC?
A: Low-risk customers: every 10 years. Medium-risk: every 8 years. High-risk: every 2 years.
